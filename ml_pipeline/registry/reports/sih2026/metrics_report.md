# Annapurna SIH-2026 - model metrics

> **All figures come from synthetic data.** `data_source = SYNTHETIC_SIH2026`. The bundle was generated by `ml_data_gen_sih2026.py` from documented mechanisms (Ratkowsky-type growth, Q10 spoilage, HACCP rules). These numbers validate the pipeline and the calibration; they are **not** evidence about a real kitchen.

Each subsystem is scored on the metric its task implies. There is deliberately no single 'accuracy' column, because averaging a forecaster's WAPE with a screen's recall would mean nothing.

## What these numbers do not establish

- No model here has seen real kitchen data.
- The food-photo classifier is untrained; `data/cv/` does not exist, so no image-based accuracy is reported at all.
- A ceiling measured on synthetic data is a ceiling on the generator, not on reality.
